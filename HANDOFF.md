## 6. Verifying in the browser pane
- Playground deep link: `?node=<sceneId>&beat=<id>&preset=<id>&device=iphone15`. Handles: `window.__nqDev` (loadNode/applyState/getState/setSpeed/pause/step/showZones/showCollision/inspectAt), `window.__nqGame`, `window.__nqStore`, `window.__nqMovie`.
- **One clock only.** `document.hidden` is true during a tool call, so the live RAF never advances inside a script. Recipe: `g.loop.sleep()`; patch every scene `s.tweens.getDelta = function () { return 16.67 * this.timeScale; }`; then drive frames. For the movie runner the loop must be **async** so its awaited frames resolve: `for (...) { g.step(T += 16.67, 16.67); await new Promise(r => setTimeout(r, 0)); }` — about 2 400 frames per 34 s tool call (the tool times out at 45 s). A full movie is ~8 such calls. Stub `MenuScene.open` only when not using the runner (the runner closes the auto-opened menu itself).
- **Driving the movie:** `__nqMovie.play()` or `.play({ scene, beat })`, then drive frames until `__nqMovie.state.status === 'done'`; read `state.report.scenes[i].steps` (id, status, reason, ms), `state.log`. `play()` queues behind a stopping run — after `.stop()` drive until status is `stopped`/`done` before the next play, and compare `report !== previousReport` to know the new run began.
- **Diagnosing a failing step:** play the scene alone; wrap `__nqMovie.driver.route` to log targets, feet and corners; sample `driver.feet()`, `driver.state()` (free, dialogueOpen, district), `WorldScene.talking/leaving/sleeping`, `player.interactionEnabled`, body velocity, and the zone overlap every few frames during the step (`__nqMovie.state.stepIndex` is 0-based; step ids `<do>_<n>` are 1-based). Blockers near a point: iterate `physics.world.colliders.getActive()` — static bodies alone include trigger/exit zones, which are not colliders. Driver privates are reachable at runtime: `driver.grid(w, target, avoidZones, withCrowd)`, `driver.resolve(w, grid, target)`. Any edit to `src/` reloads the page (Vite HMR) and kills the run — collect results first, then patch.
- Keys: `KeyboardEvent` keydown then keyup a few frames later with `code`, `key`, `keyCode` (E 69, P 80, M 77, Enter 13, Escape 27, arrows 37–40). Phone taps: mouse events on the canvas at `origin + focusable centre` (call `game.scale.updateBounds()` first — the cached bounds go stale in the scaled frame). Teleport for QA only: `p.setPosition(x, y); p.body.reset(p.x, p.y)`.

## 9. Movie Mode — the rules the eleven scenes taught the bot (all in code, keep them)
- Plan on a **2 px grid with the real 20×12 feet box** (8 px sealed the shop pavement and the gap between parked autos); the grid comes from the **player's colliders**, never every static body (trigger and exit zones are bodies too). Exits and bed/door zones are walls unless targeted, dilated by the feet box plus the steering slack; when that seals the only way, plan again without them; the crowd (unpushable) is planned around first, through second.
- Routes are string-pulled and steered as lines (perpendicular error corrected when > 1.5 px); a corridor beside an exit is as wide as the body. Stuck = no progress toward the corner for 1.2 s → sidestep onto clear ground (`freeAt`) → replan, up to five times, each adding 2 s to the deadline; an unreachable plan is retried for a few seconds (the crowd moves). The first waypoint is the start cell (the player's own cell is often dilated shut).
- Zone goals prefer a cell whose centre is inside the zone and not claimed by a sibling zone (photo spots and signal rooms read the body centre, "first zone wins"), then any overlap; after arrival the bot presses to the nearest interior point. A zone that takes the world over (workday, sleep) or opens a conversation while the player stands in it counts as arrival; a fired exit is arrival only for `exit:`/`zone:` targets, an accident otherwise. The walk's clock pauses while the world is busy.
- Conversations: `choose` picks by node id, text prefix or index with real arrow presses + Enter, then drains choice-less lines; `advance` drains; a conversation open at a near target is the next step's. `interact` waits out the 80 ms re-enable after a close, presses again once, and fails `wrong_npc` if a neighbour answered (NPC goals stand nearer the target than any other NPC). A negotiation's accept option is matched by its `acceptText` prefix.
- Sleep: wait for the previous night's fade; if the body still touches the bed zone, walk through the planner to a spot clear of it (the watcher needs a real exit); then enter. Phone: `app` taps the home tile (back out of a thread/reel first), `tap` taps a focusable by id, `scroll` presses down, `close` presses P. The auto-opened pause menu is closed inside every wait.
- Generator (`scripts/generate-walkthroughs.ts`): prereq flags + `entry.after` → objectives in order; pre-entry objectives (before the first in-district one) are skipped with a note; flags resolve by trigger here → sleep-driven timers (`hotel_slept_once`, `pg_notice_due`) → signal rooms / reels / photo sets → conversations here (including enter-branch flags earned first, and the open conversation continued) → another district through exits (the objective's `destination` preferred); a quest already complete in the prereq never expects its `on_complete` flags; dialogue objectives satisfied by a later purchase from the same NPC are merged.
