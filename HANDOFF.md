## 9. Movie Mode — rules the scenes taught the bot (all in code)
- Plan on a 2 px grid with the real 20×12 feet box, from the player's colliders only. Exits are soft walls (dropped when they seal the way); **live world-taking zones (bed, office door/workday, guitar lane) are hard walls unless they are or overlap the target**; the crowd is planned around first, through second.
- Stuck (no progress for 1.2 s) → sidestep → wait (longer each time) → replan, **up to 10 times**. A zone that opens the rhythm lane or a conversation, or takes the world over, counts as arrival.
- `choose` steps through choice-less lines until the options appear, then picks by node id / text prefix / index with real arrows + Enter. Negotiations: `planHaggle` counters, then the accept text.
- Sleep: clear the bed zone first. Phone: `app` taps the home tile; `tap` a focusable id (`jodi:*`, `news:*`, `camera:snap`, `reel:*`). `travel`: M → tap the node (positions from `MapScene.inspect()`) → F.
