# NAMMA OUTBREAK — Founder Profile & Future Vision v1.0
> A model of how the creator (amitesh) thinks, so any Claude session can predict his preferences and propose ideas he'd actually like. Built from the full design conversation.

## 1. Hats he wore (he is a one-person studio brain)
Game designer · narrative writer · systems architect · principal software engineer · art director · product manager · growth/marketing strategist · business/monetization lead. He context-switches between these fluidly and expects Claude to keep up.

## 2. How he thinks (observed patterns)
- **Realism is the hook.** Rupees not points, PGs not "houses", fuel not infinite, UPI that dies. If a choice makes the world more authentically Bangalore, he picks it.
- **Systems over content.** Prefers one reusable mechanism (5 mission types, one stability number, atomic tokens, one WorldScene) to many bespoke pieces. Hates duplication and rework.
- **Agile, phase-by-phase, audit-driven.** "Don't build 5 scenes then edit 5." One thing, review, learn his taste, continue. Values a record of decisions so neither party drifts.
- **Emotion + meaning.** The identity-fade arc, Ravi mirroring the player, "life goes on even in apocalypse." Wants players to *feel*, not just play.
- **Marketing is designed in, not bolted on.** Controversy/ragebait beats, City Pulse moment-marketing, shareable cards, sponsors — all native to the design.
- **Ethical guardrails matter to him.** No pay-to-win, no dark patterns, blur-the-line to avoid defamation/politics, legal hygiene on brands/institutions.
- **Non-forcing / discovery.** Google-Maps reroute, optional events, curiosity as the driver. He trusts the player.
- **Learns by analogy & correction.** References games he's played (Pokémon LeafGreen, Zelter) and real life; refines by auditing concrete output, not abstract specs. Explain new terms plainly once, he adopts them fast.
- **Thinks about scale and the next thing early** (other cities, revenue, presentation website) while still disciplined about finishing the slice first.

## 3. Likely future moves (predict & pre-empt)
- **More cities, same engine:** Mumbai ("Aamchi Outbreak"), Delhi, Hyderabad — swap atom/organism layer (ADR-60). Keep this seam clean.
- **Live-ops cadence:** weekly City Pulse, festival events, seasonal City Moments → a content calendar.
- **Creator/UGC angle:** let fans submit City Pulse-style events or pockets (moderated) — matches his "people connect" + marketing instincts.
- **Short-form virality:** in-game moments auto-clipped for Reels/Shorts (his Reels/ragebait awareness).
- **Sponsor marketplace:** self-serve banner slots by heatmap; local Bangalore businesses first.
- **Platform:** PWA first → later wrap for Play Store (TWA). Possible regional-language full localisation (Kannada/Hindi/Telugu/Tamil).
- **Narrative expansion:** Starters B/C (bus/train), branching endings, a Ravi-POV side campaign.
- He will likely want **analytics dashboards** and a **playtest telemetry** view (local-first, privacy-light).

## 4. Ideas to propose to him (aligned with his taste)
1. **"Namma Score"** city-reputation meta that persists across cities (ties identity theme + sharing).
2. **Weather-driven gameplay** from real Bangalore weather API (rain today → waterlogging in-game) — a safe, non-political City Pulse input he'd love.
3. **Monsoon season event** where the storm-drain spread mechanic becomes a seasonal hard-mode.
4. **"Last Bus" rescue missions** — timed, emotional, reuse deliver handler.
5. **Postcard economy**: shareable cards unlock cosmetic tokens → organic growth loop within ethical rules.
6. **Accessibility as a feature, marketed** (he values ethics + differentiation).

## 5. How to work with him (operating manual for Claude)
- Lead with a one-line reaction, then substance. Keep voice replies short; put depth in files.
- Always name the relevant ADR when something he says conflicts with a past decision; let him choose.
- Offer options as yes/no when he's deciding fast.
- Default to: new file for new concerns, update for living docs (TASKS/MEMORY/AUDIT_LOG/DECISIONS). Never overwrite settled design silently.
- Respect the phase gates and the "ask for references at build time" rule.
- When unsure, bias toward realism, reuse, emotion, and ethical marketing — his four north stars.

## 6. One-line mission (his, distilled)
"Make the Bangalore I actually lived in — then let it fall — in a way that's honest, reusable, and impossible not to share."
